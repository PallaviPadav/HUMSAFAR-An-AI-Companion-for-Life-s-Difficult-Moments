### 💗 A message for someone going through marital struggles

“When your marriage becomes a source of pain instead of comfort, it is easy to lose yourself in endless questions, expectations, and disappointments. But please remember that your life is bigger than the problems you are facing today. Give yourself permission to feel hurt without letting the hurt define you. Focus on your well-being, rebuild your confidence, nurture the relationships that support you, and take things one step at a time. If both partners are willing to listen, take responsibility, and make genuine changes, a relationship may heal. And whatever the outcome, you still deserve a life filled with dignity, peace, and happiness.”

One thing to remember: You do not have to decide the entire future of your relationship today. Start by asking yourself what you need to feel safe, respected, and emotionally well. Your well-being matters, too.
